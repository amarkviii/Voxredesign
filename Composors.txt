Primarily a concert series in its past Composer's Voice has produced more than 150 concerts in New York City. The concert series has presented more than 150 concerts in New York City and through out the world.  <a href="http://www.voxnovus.com/composersvoice/calendar/">A calendar and history of Composer's Voice concerts can be found here.</a>. </p> <p> Works are chosen from a wide range of contemporary composers and performed by dedicated musicians devoted to new music. The goal is to give an opportunity for the composer to have a forum in which they can express their own aesthic or personal "voice." Composer's Voice does this in several ways: </p> 		 		<div id="content"> - <b>Frequent Performances</b> - Composer's Voice has presented more than 150 performances in New York City and around the world.  		</div> 		<div id="content"> - <b>Calls for Scores</b> - Composer's Voice often presents musicians and ensembles who are looking for works to fit their instrumentation. Composer's Voice disseminates these calls to its members and the public and organizes calls for scores to fit the musicians needs.  <a href="http://www.voxnovus.com/composersvoice/calls">Check out new calls for scores and calls for works for Composer's Voice here.</a> 		</div> 		<div id="content"> - <b>Talented Musicians</b> - Composer's Voice specifically seeks, presents, and promotes talented musicians who embrace new music.  The project empowers these champions of music from living composers.  Composer's Voice does this by administering calls for scores, recordings, performances, and networking. 		</div>. 		<div id="content"> - <b>Concert Curation</b> - Understanding the difficult environment that new music experiences, Composer's Voice also presents opportunities for individuals and organizations to curate and organize performance. The goal is to strengthen and empower the field with successful concertizing strategies to further propegate new music. Composer's Voice also seeks to collaborate with musicians, composers, curators, and other new music organizations to creat opportunities for the composer and new music.  		</div>. 	</div>. 	<div id="spacer"> 	</div> 	<img src="http://www.voxnovus.com/img/Composers_Voice-line.png" style="width:100%">. <div id="spacer"> </div>. 	<div id="text_block">.  		 		<h2>Composer's Voice </h2><h3>Director</h2>. 		  <img src="http://www.voxnovus.com/member/pic/Robert_Voisey_13.png" height="50" style="float: left;"> 		  <p> <a href="http://www.voxnovus.com/composer/Robert_Voisey.htm">Robert Voisey</a> is the Executive Director of Vox Novus, a new music production and promotion company.
