Fifteen Minutes of Fame features 15 one-minute works by different composers, written for specific performers or ensembles.

This project brings a wide variety of new music by living composers to audiences eager to hear what is being created in today's music scene.

"...the 15 Minutes of Fame project of Vox Novus is a godsend. It organizes competitions whereby ensembles commission worldwide calls for submissions of approximately one-minute pieces, and then select their 15 favorites for premieres..."
- Seth Gilman, NY Music Culture Examiner

Since the beginning of the project, more than 100 calls for scores have been issued, ranging from solo instruments to full ensembles. A diverse variety of instruments has been featured, from harp to oud to melodica.

"The obvious strength of that approach is that it allows many composers to gain exposure in a short time span. The pieces are performed in quick succession, each with its own character, creating a thrilling kaleidoscope of contemporary idioms."
- Damjan Rakonjac, Artificialist

Over 600 composers from more than 30 countries have had works premiered. Past performances are archived and can be viewed on the Video Page.

Fifteen Minutes of Fame was created by producer and composer Robert Voisey to give composers the opportunity to have their works performed worldwide.
